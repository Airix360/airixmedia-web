# Living Lagos Creative Studio

Status: **Approved redesign direction**  
Applies after: Phase 01 baseline preservation  
Primary CTA: **Discuss a project**

## Intended impression

The Airix Media website should feel like a premium international creative studio: energetic, young, ambitious, broad, full-service, expensive, and highly produced. Creativity leads; technical capability becomes clear through the work. The experience should contain surprising moments, while African identity reveals itself progressively rather than becoming a superficial motif.

## Opening hero

The opening should combine:

- An interactive Lagos-inspired digital landscape
- A full-screen cinematic showreel
- Curiosity before explanation
- Real interface fragments
- Interactive movement without sound
- One strongly art-directed opening appearance

The opening hero must not contain OJS imagery. It must not place prominent “Airix Media” text in the first frame. It should make the studio feel authored before it explains the service catalogue.

## Lagos inspiration

References may draw from Lagos energy, movement, ambition, density, colour, nightlife, commerce, water, signage, architecture, bridges, ferries, traffic, markets, and street typography. These references must be recognisable but art-directed. They should shape composition, motion, rhythm, typography, and transitions rather than appear as a checklist of decorative symbols.

## People

People may appear only in controlled atmospheric roles:

- Silhouettes
- Crowds
- Motion blur
- Street-life fragments
- Human scale

AI-generated faces and generic lifestyle portraits must not become the visual centre.

## Visual language

- Sans-serif and serif pairing
- Architectural typography
- Selective oversized type
- A denser, more visual composition than the rejected design
- Warm ivory instead of clinical white
- Warm charcoal instead of pure black
- One primary Airix accent instead of five service colours
- Selective glass, not glass everywhere
- Fine grain
- Controlled halftone or dither
- Full-colour project imagery
- Cinematic transitions
- Surprising interaction with a clear narrative purpose

The design must not revert to empty monochrome sections, generic technical grids, floating feature labels, fake browser frames, repeated giant headings, or abstract technology imagery used in place of proof.

## Content priorities

Within the first minute, visitors must understand:

1. Airix creates premium websites and digital experiences.
2. Airix builds custom business systems, automation, and integrations.
3. Airix has specialist scholarly-publishing and OJS expertise.

Projects should carry more of the homepage than service descriptions. Authentic project imagery, interface states, outcomes, process evidence, and approved artefacts should do the explanatory work.

## Calls to action

The primary call to action is:

> Discuss a project

WhatsApp must not appear in the main navigation.

The qualified project-brief flow may remain the conversion mechanism behind the CTA, but the visitor-facing entry point must feel consultative rather than transactional.

## Placeholder policy

Airix Labs projects may use clearly labelled internal placeholders during supervised review. Placeholder products must never be presented as launched, completed, publicly available, or client-approved.

Internal review states, approval warnings, and placeholder labels must not appear in the public visitor experience. Publication filtering must exclude unapproved material rather than display a warning beside it.

## Direction boundary

This brief approves a concept direction, not the current interface and not any unreviewed implementation. Future phases must present committed screenshots and decisions under `design-review/` and obtain owner review at the defined gates.
