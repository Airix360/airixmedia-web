# Owner review

## How to review

1. Open `/__design_lab` on a desktop browser.
2. Enter A, B and C in order; do not choose from the directory thumbnail alone.
3. For each, move the pointer, scroll to **Position**, then **First proof**.
4. Repeat on a phone or at 390 px width.
5. Enable **Reduce motion** in the operating system and reload each opening.
6. Use Tab to verify navigation and action focus.

## Evaluation questions

- Does this feel specifically like Airix rather than a technology template?
- Is Lagos present through operating logic and material—not costume?
- Does the city idea hand off clearly to digital work?
- Does Build / Run / Rescue remain intelligible?
- Would a serious buyer trust the system after the expressive opening?
- Does mobile feel authored rather than collapsed?
- Is the reduced-motion opening equivalent, not diminished?
- What single element should survive into the next phase?
- What must be removed even if the concept advances?
