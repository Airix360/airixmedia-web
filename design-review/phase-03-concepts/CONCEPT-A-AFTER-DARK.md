# Concept A — After Dark

## Thesis

Digital operations become most visible when something must keep working without ceremony. After Dark begins with the city as active infrastructure, then reveals Airix as the partner connecting, observing and sustaining the system.

## Structure and behaviour

1. Full-screen abstract night network: road lines, paired bridge arcs, lagoon mass and live signal points.
2. Small directional statement rather than a giant first-frame headline.
3. Scroll transition into the core proposition.
4. Dark operating interface labelled **Concept interface**.
5. Signal-yellow Build / Run / Rescue close.

Pointer movement shifts a low-intensity light field. Scroll adds controlled depth to the city scene. Reduced motion holds the scene static and reveals the proposition and both actions immediately.

## Provisional typography

- **Sans:** Barlow Condensed — directional labels, navigation and compact opening statement.
- **Serif:** Literata — proposition, evidence and explanatory copy.
- **Source / licence:** Google Fonts and upstream repositories; SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Fit:** architectural and sign-led without redrawing actual signs.
- **Weakness:** the condensed face quickly becomes theatrical when enlarged or repeated.
- **Mobile rule:** short sans labels only; long reading remains in Literata.

## Provisional palette and contrast

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asphalt | `#0d0e0f` | Opening and footer |
| Lagoon black | `#151a1d` | Proof surface |
| Signal yellow | `#ffd400` | Route signals and primary action |
| Headlamp | `#f4f0df` | Primary text |
| Tail light | `#ef4c35` | Rare state cue |

Headlamp on Asphalt and Asphalt on Signal Yellow are the primary high-contrast pairs. Tail light is never used for reading text. The palette relates to night navigation and operational signals, not neon nightlife styling.

## Risk to test

Premium darkness can drift into “creative technology studio” convention. The deciding question is whether the Lagos infrastructure and accountable operations handoff make it specific enough to Airix.
