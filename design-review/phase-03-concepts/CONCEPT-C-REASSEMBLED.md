# Concept C — Reassembled

## Thesis

Airix often begins with something partially working: an inherited platform, an abandoned build or operations spread across tools. Reassembled makes that reality visible, then resolves fragments into one accountable operating view.

## Structure and behaviour

1. Full-screen paper grid, generated scraps, scaffold rails and halftone field.
2. One controlled collision between proposition and system fragment.
3. Clean serif proposition after the opening.
4. Dark proof field where fragments resolve into one light interface.
5. Tarp-blue Build / Run / Rescue close.

Pointer movement creates a subtle local print-light response. Scroll transitions from spatial collision to ordered content. Reduced motion freezes the assembled opening and makes the proposition and actions available in the same viewport.

## Provisional typography

- **Sans:** Archivo Black — painted instruction, section marks and operating verbs.
- **Serif:** Bodoni Moda — proposition and editorial counterweight.
- **Source / licence:** Google Fonts and upstream repositories; SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Fit:** physical construction and print tension without borrowing a named fashion identity.
- **Weakness:** both faces are forceful; undisciplined repetition would become costume.
- **Mobile rule:** display scale contracts sharply and the second viewport returns to conventional reading order.

## Provisional palette and contrast

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copy paper | `#f2eedf` | Opening and interface |
| Process ink | `#11100d` | Text and scaffold rails |
| Paint red | `#e0442e` | Collision point and action |
| Tarp blue | `#2458a6` | Structural fragment and close |
| Chalk yellow | `#f0c928` | Annotation |

Process Ink on Copy Paper and Copy Paper on Process Ink are the reading pairs. White text is used on Paint Red and Tarp Blue at display weight. Yellow holds black display text only. The palette relates to copied matter, painted instructions and temporary construction materials.

## Risk to test

The opening is intentionally the most expressive. Review whether it communicates “rescue and reassembly” or merely looks like a graphic-design exercise.
