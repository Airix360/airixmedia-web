# Decisions

## Fixed for this phase

1. The design lab is additive and private-by-convention: literal `/__design_lab` URLs, no public links, no sitemap inclusion and explicit `noindex, nofollow`.
2. The three concepts use one shared semantic content spine but different composition, typography, palette, material and motion logic.
3. All illustrative content is generated with CSS and inline SVG. No reference asset was downloaded, scraped, copied or hotlinked.
4. Generated work is labelled **Concept interface** inside the design lab only. No client name, testimonial, metric or performance claim is invented.
5. Motion uses the existing `motion` dependency plus CSS, SVG and browser pointer events. No animation, video, smooth-scroll, WebGL or component package was added.
6. Reduced motion is not merely slower motion: the proposition and both primary actions are present in the opening viewport as a static alternative.
7. After Dark opens on the city system rather than a dominant headline. This was corrected after live browser inspection.
8. In Motion uses a mobile-specific composition that removes the route placard at narrow widths and protects headline legibility.
9. Reassembled permits one controlled collision in the opening because assembly tension is the concept itself; reading order becomes conventional immediately after the opening.

## Not decided

- No winning concept.
- No production homepage structure.
- No production font family or palette.
- No new logo or refinement of the existing logo.
- No client work, photography or case-study material for publication.
- No final motion duration or production performance budget.
