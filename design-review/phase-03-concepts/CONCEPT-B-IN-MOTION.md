# Concept B — In Motion

## Thesis

Lagos commerce is not a static catalogue. Orders, payments, people and decisions move through visible handoffs. In Motion turns that current into an Airix proposition about making movement legible and operable.

## Structure and behaviour

1. Daylight field with market verbs, a sun disc, abstract ferry wakes and a route placard.
2. Large proposition shaped by a variable-width sans.
3. Scroll into a quieter, serif-led explanation.
4. High-contrast lagoon-blue proof field and light operating interface.
5. Fast yellow Build / Run / Rescue close.

Pointer movement provides a restrained local response. The mobile opening removes the placard and protects the headline with an ivory inline field instead of shrinking the desktop composition. Reduced motion presents a static daylight poster plus the proposition and actions.

## Provisional typography

- **Sans:** Anybody — variable-width movement, signage and display.
- **Serif:** Bitter — explanatory text and controlled pauses.
- **Source / licence:** Google Fonts and upstream repositories; SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **Fit:** energetic and practical without becoming a tourism or sport campaign.
- **Weakness:** extreme width settings become cartoonish.
- **Mobile rule:** width axis is moderated and words are protected from arbitrary breaks.

## Provisional palette and contrast

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market ivory | `#f4ead2` | Opening and interface |
| Concrete | `#d7ccb5` | Supporting neutral |
| Danfo yellow | `#f2c800` | Movement marker |
| Lagoon blue | `#006e78` | Proof field and route |
| Pepper red | `#d64125` | Primary action |
| Ink | `#171611` | Primary text |

Ink on Market Ivory, Market Ivory on Lagoon Blue, and light text on Pepper Red carry reading contrast. Yellow is a spatial signal, not body text. The colour relationship comes from moving identifiers, water and warm built surfaces rather than a literal bus illustration.

## Risk to test

The energy may make Airix feel more like a cultural brand than an operations partner. Review whether the work reveal restores enough technical confidence without losing movement.
