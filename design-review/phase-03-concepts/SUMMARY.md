# Phase 03 — Living Lagos concept interpretations

## Outcome

Three isolated, live concept routes were built for owner review:

- **A — After Dark:** a city-first night infrastructure study.
- **B — In Motion:** a daylight commerce and movement study.
- **C — Reassembled:** a controlled street-workshop collage study.

The neutral review index is available at `/__design_lab`. The routes are deliberately absent from public navigation and the sitemap, and every design-lab page emits `noindex, nofollow` metadata.

## Scope protection

The public homepage, public header and footer, service routes, pricing, publishing opening, production infrastructure, Cloudflare, DNS, Dell, portal, tunnels and deployment configuration were not redesigned or deployed. The lab uses its own CSS module, typography variables and components.

## What this phase decides

Nothing has been selected for production. The package makes three different structural hypotheses reviewable in a browser, on mobile, with keyboard focus and with reduced motion. Owner review should identify what to carry forward, what to combine cautiously and what to reject.

## Evidence

- 27 Playwright screenshots: 9 per concept.
- Three silent WebM walkthroughs at 1280 × 800, each below 8 MB.
- Desktop, mobile and combined contact sheets.
- Independent Hallmark audit recorded after implementation.
- Automated checks for route isolation, metadata, sitemap exclusion, critical actions and reduced motion.
