# Decisions

## Scope

- Kept work entirely on `redesign/living-lagos`.
- Preserved the Phase 3 review package byte-for-byte.
- Kept the design lab private, noindex and absent from public navigation and sitemap output.
- Used no new dependency and made no deployment or infrastructure change.

## Architecture

- Replaced the shared `ConceptInterface` with `AfterDarkExperience`, `InMotionExperience` and `ReassembledExperience`.
- Shared only two small utilities: the review caption and project-discussion link.
- Kept the concept index separate from all concept-specific CSS.

## Experience choices

- After Dark uses an edge route legend, a sticky city opening, sequential full-bleed responsibility scenes and a quiet letter-like close.
- In Motion uses a destination strip, manual stage controls, a horizontal handoff model, two service pathways and a bold dispatch close.
- Reassembled uses a scaffold rail, material fragments, a broken-system diagnosis, scroll-linked assembly and a calm accountability close.
- Generated proof content covers websites, commerce, payment, business operations, portals, publishing and continuity without client names or fabricated metrics.
- “Concept material” appears once per concept as discreet review metadata.

## Motion and accessibility

- After Dark: contrast and signal illumination.
- In Motion: manual directional handoffs; no autoplay.
- Reassembled: fragment translation and rotational lock-in.
- Reduced motion removes spatial animation and preserves equivalent static proof.
- Navigation remains keyboard reachable, focus-visible and touch operable.

## Provisional status

No typography or colour decision is approved by this phase. Tokens are formalised only to make each study internally coherent and reviewable.
