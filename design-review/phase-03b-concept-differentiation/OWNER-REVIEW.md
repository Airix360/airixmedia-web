# Owner review

Review the live routes, recordings and contact sheets before choosing. This is a behavioural-direction decision; typography and colour are not yet approved.

## Routes

- `/__design_lab/after-dark`
- `/__design_lab/in-motion`
- `/__design_lab/reassembled`

## For each concept

1. Use its navigation, not only the browser scroll bar.
2. Reach the first transition and unique proof mechanism.
3. Review the final CTA and closing tone.
4. Repeat at 390 px or on a phone.
5. Enable reduced motion and confirm the proof remains understandable.

## Decision prompts

### After Dark

- Does patient darkness communicate operational confidence, or feel too quiet?
- Is the Build / Operate / Recover corridor a strong Airix story?
- Should the city infrastructure remain this abstract?

### In Motion

- Does manual dispatch make the operating model easier to understand?
- Does its daylight energy feel credible for both digital systems and publishing?
- Is this the strongest conversion-oriented direction?

### Reassembled

- Does the inherited-system and rescue narrative feel most specific to Airix?
- Is the workshop material language controlled enough to feel premium?
- Does the assembly proof explain accountability clearly?
