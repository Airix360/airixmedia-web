# Phase 03B — Living Lagos concept differentiation

Phase 03B replaces the rejected shared agency-page skeleton with three independent browser experiences while preserving each concept’s strongest opening idea.

- **After Dark** is a slow, dark infrastructure corridor. A compact route legend reveals Build, Operate and Recover scenes through illumination and depth.
- **In Motion** is a warm, manually dispatched journey. Visitors move an idea through Experience, Payment, Operation and Support, then choose between digital-systems and publishing pathways.
- **Reassembled** is a workshop and scaffold system. Disconnected website, commerce, payment, portal, publishing and continuity fragments resolve into one accountable operating layer.

The public homepage and all production-facing routes remain untouched. The Phase 3 archive remains unchanged. Typography and colour are still provisional; this package asks the owner to choose a behavioural direction, not approve a final identity.

## Evidence

- 36 still captures: desktop, mobile and reduced-motion states
- Three silent WebM interaction recordings, each below 8 MB
- Desktop, mobile and combined contact sheets
- Automated checks for structural distinction, reduced motion, mobile reading, lab isolation and public-route protection
- Independent Hallmark audit with no critical findings

## Outcome

The concepts no longer share navigation, page macrostructure, proof mechanics, motion recipes, section counts or closing treatments. Phase 3B is ready for owner comparison; it is not a production-design approval.
