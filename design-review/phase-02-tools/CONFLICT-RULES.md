# Design-tool conflict rules

These rules are non-negotiable.

1. Never invoke all design skills for one task.
2. One tool leads each pass.
3. Other tools may audit only after the lead pass is complete.
4. Hallmark and `gpt-taste` must not simultaneously redesign the same component.
5. Impeccable refines an approved direction; it does not originate it.
6. shadcn handles functional primitives; it does not style the marketing site.
7. Generated concept images are references, not client evidence.
8. No tool may modify production infrastructure.
9. No tool may publish or deploy.
10. No tool may silently replace the approved Living Lagos brief.
11. No tool may reintroduce empty monochrome expanses, repeated giant headings, monospace eyebrow labels everywhere, floating feature chips, fake browser frames, five service colours, generic technical grids, gradient-heavy SaaS imagery, or excessive rounded cards.
12. Every implementation pass must be followed by screenshots and an independent audit.
13. Rejected concepts must remain archived but excluded from production builds.
14. Visitor-facing pages must never expose internal approval labels.

## Pass ordering

1. The owner or phase brief names one lead.
2. The lead completes a bounded pass and stops.
3. Playwright captures deterministic evidence.
4. Hallmark or another authorised critic reviews the completed pass independently.
5. Impeccable may refine only after the direction is approved.
6. The owner approves, rejects, or requests another bounded pass.

## Conflict resolution

- The Living Lagos brief outranks third-party defaults and theme catalogues.
- Authentic approved project material outranks generated references.
- Accessibility and evidence safety outrank visual novelty.
- When two tools disagree, preserve the current approved state and document the disagreement for owner review.
- No tool may resolve a conflict by silently changing production code, dependencies, or infrastructure.
