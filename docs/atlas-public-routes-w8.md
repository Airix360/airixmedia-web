# Atlas Public Routes W8

## Canonical navigation

Work, Services, Publishing, Atlas, Studio and Discuss a Project. Emergency Support
and Client Portal are direct utilities.

## Route families

- Lagos: `/`, `/work`, `/work/[slug]`, `/services`, digital experiences and business systems.
- Oyo: `/publishing`, `/publishing/ojs`, universities, journal platforms,
  editorial support, plugins and pricing.
- Rivers: managed infrastructure, support, emergency, knowledge base, status,
  security and service levels.
- Edo: `/studio`.
- Kaduna: `/open-source` and `/open-source/[slug]`.
- Plateau: `/atlas`, `/insights`, `/insights/[slug]`, `/resources`, `/resources/[slug]`.
- Ogun: `/discuss`, `/contact`, `/book`.
- Shared: `/legal`.

## Compatibility redirects

- `/systems*` → `/work*`
- `/company` → `/studio`
- `/start-a-project` and `/project-brief` → `/discuss`

The sitemap contains canonical English routes only. Locale routes remain available
as the earlier partial implementation, but translation completion and canonical
locale review are W9 blockers.
