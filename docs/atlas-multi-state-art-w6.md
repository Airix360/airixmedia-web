## Cultural and architectural reasoning

Edo is translated through contemporary making and stewardship: layered thresholds, shaded courtyards, disciplined material work, restrained mineral and red-earth references, timber, paper, clay, contemporary masonry, visible review, record keeping, and continuity. Studio uses an open making compound; Trust uses a distinct civic record hall. Sacred, royal, archaeological, and museum objects are excluded.

Kaduna is translated through contemporary engineering and structured learning: practical civic/educational architecture, dry-season light, rail and route geometry, fabrication bays, test rigs, documentation, repair, and measured iteration. Costume, religious shorthand, emirate fantasy, military imagery, and generic desert/savannah scenery are excluded.

The shared Atlas language uses contemporary Nigerian subjects, grounded human scale, Gongbi-influenced precision, fine deep-brown or charcoal linework, simplified believable architecture, warm paper material, flat screen-print/risograph colour, restrained shading, inhabited monumental composition, discreet wayfinding, and intentional copy space.

## Approved vocabulary and prohibited shortcuts

Approved vocabulary: working compounds, record halls, courtyards, thresholds, material libraries, review tables, archives, prototype benches, technical workshops, route geometry, repair stations, documentation, anonymous people working naturally, cream/umber/charcoal, restrained state accents, and believable contemporary materials.

Prohibited shortcuts: costume or tourism display; palace, ancient-city, desert, savannah, military, or religious fantasy; masks, warriors, royal regalia, sacred objects, decorative flags, pan-African pattern dumping, padlocks, shields, badges, handshakes, fake certificates, fake readable text, generated logos, fake language, photorealism, glossy CGI, cinematic 3D, cyberpunk, neon, holograms, floating screens, flying vehicles, hyper-detailed faces, ceremonial posing, watermarks, dashboards, and generic innovation-hub imagery.

## Generation method

OpenAI’s built-in image-generation capability produced six candidates on 2026-07-14: a separate desktop and mobile master for each of Studio, Trust, and Labs. Prompts specified the shared Atlas language, state-specific environment, working activity, copy-safe negative space, and extensive negative controls. The generated PNGs were retained as source masters, then resized and encoded locally into WebP desktop, tablet, mobile, and thumbnail derivatives. No stock imagery or Lagos district scene was substituted. No generated asset is marked approved.

## Known defects and approval requirements

- All candidates require owner, cultural, and architectural review by people with relevant Edo or Kaduna context.
- Small hands, fingers, tools, prototype components, document stacks, cabinets, rail geometry, and background figures require close artefact inspection.
- The Studio mobile blue-hour field is stronger than desktop; cross-composition continuity needs review.
- The Trust tree and record hall and the Labs rail yard are fictional composites, not verified places.
- No separable supporting object, person, atmosphere, or motion layers were generated.
- Copy-safe zones work in the current review compositions but require approval at laptop/tablet breakpoints.
- Before promotion, approve cultural treatment, architectural plausibility, anatomy, generated-text absence, crop, colour, art direction, alt text, source-master retention, performance budget, rights/provenance record, and final registry IDs.
- Candidate status must remain until explicit owner approval; passing tests and review screenshots do not promote any image.
