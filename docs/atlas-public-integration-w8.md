# Atlas Public Integration W8

## Boundary

W8 replaces the public English prototype on `build/atlas-public-integration-w8`
only. It does not merge, deploy, change DNS, connect production integrations or
approve candidate artwork.

The implementation uses the seven-chapter homepage, clear public navigation,
state-specific internal routes, authentic-proof rules and local-only functional
fallbacks. Internal W0–W7.1 review routes remain under `/internal/`, excluded by
robots and absent from public navigation.

## Visual and interaction thesis

Atlas is one Nigerian operating world rather than a sequence of page templates.
Each route family receives geography and infrastructure native to its assigned
state. Ordinary scrolling, direct links and brief route handoffs preserve usability.
Generated art establishes place; verified interfaces and repositories establish proof.

## Carried limitations

All art remains candidate; owner, cultural, architectural, landmark, anatomy,
crop, provenance and rights review remain pending. Public integration is not launch
approval. Legal, analytics, consent, CRM, calendar, status and translation work
remain W9 decisions.
