# Rivers Public Artwork W8

## Candidate family

`ILL-0110` Managed Infrastructure, `ILL-0111` Support/Continuity and `ILL-0112`
Emergency Recovery each have independently authored desktop and mobile masters and
four compressed runtime derivatives.

## Native recognition anchors

The scenes use Bonny River/creek geography, humid mangrove edges, Onne-informed
quays, maintenance jetties, service boats, navigation buoys, utility masts, concrete
service structures and road/water handoffs. These are based on Nigerian Ports
Authority records for Onne Port's estuary, berths, road and inland-waterway links,
service-boat and maintenance infrastructure.

The environments are fictional composites and reproduce no private operator.
Oil-industry glamour, spills, damage spectacle and militarised imagery are excluded.
The distant tanks in the Support desktop master are an explicit owner-review risk.
