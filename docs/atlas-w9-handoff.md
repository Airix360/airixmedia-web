# Atlas W9 Handoff

W8 is a public-integration candidate, not launch approval.

## Required decisions and approvals

- Owner approval for every page composition, copy family and artwork candidate.
- Cultural/architectural review for Lagos, Oyo, Rivers, Edo, Kaduna, Plateau and Ogun.
- Legal entity, jurisdiction, privacy, consent, terms, retention and data-processing review.
- Client/project permission and evidence, especially Airix Food, AJTMBR and KU scope/outcomes.
- Production CRM/contact endpoint, transactional email, spam controls and failure states.
- Calendar provider, host, duration, availability, cancellation and privacy rules.
- Status, analytics and consent integrations.
- Human French and Portuguese translation; locale metadata/navigation review.
- Final SEO titles/descriptions, structured data and search-console verification.
- Manual WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard, screen-reader, contrast and zoom review.
- Lighthouse remediation where targets are missed on production-equivalent hosting.
- Source-master Git LFS/creative archive policy and complete rights/provenance register.

## Artwork still candidate

All W6–W8 images remain candidate. Small hands, faces, papers, tools, rails,
vehicles, boats, bridge geometry and abstract marks need close inspection. The Oyo
open-source mobile rock form and Rivers Support desktop industrial tanks need an
explicit retain/regenerate decision.

## Launch blockers

No owner/cultural/legal approval, incomplete translations, incomplete project
evidence, unverified production forms/booking/status/analytics, pending manual
accessibility review and candidate artwork status. W9 must use staging, production-
equivalent monitoring, security headers, analytics/consent tests, deployment health
checks and rollback rehearsal before any launch decision.
