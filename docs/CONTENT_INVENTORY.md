# Content Inventory

## Reusable facts

- Contact details and portal URL
- Current owner-confirmed OJS package amounts
- Existing service and policy concepts, subject to review
- Public repository names and URLs
- Public project and venture URLs

## Review-required material

- Client logos and screenshots
- Testimonials and attributed quotes
- Quantified outcomes, project counts, response history, and uptime claims
- Case-study challenges, responsibilities, and ongoing roles
- Legal entity names and jurisdictions

## Excluded patterns

No fabricated metrics, testimonials, client results, team members, certifications, partnerships, awards, or direct rankings promises.
