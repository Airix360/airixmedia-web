# Current Site Audit

The current site provides useful facts, prices, URLs, policies, and publishing history but must not guide the new layout or company-wide identity.

## Primary issues

- OJS dominates the homepage and company story.
- Service naming and page titles alternate between Airix Media, Airix Media Studio, and Airix OJS Studio.
- Testimonials and measurable case-study outcomes require evidence and permission review.
- Generic response-time commitments are hard-coded into pages and packages.
- At least one OJS service page applies a dollar symbol to naira-scale values.
- The site promises a typical contact response time that is not tied to a purchased package.

## Migration principle

Preserve valuable indexed URLs through redirects while replacing visual design, information architecture, copy hierarchy, and content modelling.
