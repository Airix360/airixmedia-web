## Native recognition anchors

The hidden-label package combines Ibadan's layered hill-and-valley form, restrained
brown-roof fabric, low-rise university/library architecture, shaded colonnades,
laterite retaining walls, print/archive rooms and contemporary editorial activity.
The infrastructure carries manuscripts, issues and records through the composition.

Research used the University of Ibadan, Kenneth Dike Library, University of Ibadan
journals and repository as environmental references. Every scene is a fictional
composite and implies no institutional endorsement.
