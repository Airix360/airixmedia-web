# Atlas launch decision — W9

Classification: **Not ready**.

The W9 branch provides a technically stronger public prototype and a complete private review workspace. Recommended next milestone: owner/discipline review of the W9 evidence package, followed by a separately authorised staging-readiness sprint that resolves every severity-one blocker. Do not merge, deploy, change DNS or begin post-W9 implementation from this decision.
