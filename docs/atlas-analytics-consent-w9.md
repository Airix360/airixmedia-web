# Atlas analytics and consent — W9

No analytics, advertising, session replay, chat, embedded calendar or consent platform loads in W9. Therefore the prototype does not display a decorative cookie banner or simulate consent storage.

Before introducing Umami or another provider, the owner and legal reviewer must approve: precise measurement purposes; events and properties; whether identifiers/cookies are used; legal basis by target region; consent-before-load behaviour where required; retention; IP handling; processor/subprocessor details; opt-out and consent withdrawal; development/staging exclusion; and access/incident ownership. Data minimisation excludes free-text project details, email addresses, full URLs containing secrets and artwork-review decisions from analytics.
