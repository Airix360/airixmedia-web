# R3 artwork production specification

## Locked standard and pair rule

Every asset is one standalone Gongbi-influenced illustration: brush-and-ink outlines, fine architectural detail, warm paper surface, restrained cream/ochre/clay/muted-green/brown/blue palette, believable contemporary Nigerian people and infrastructure. No photorealism, CGI, stock-photo styling, generic motifs, fantasy/European architecture, readable generated text, signs, logos, interfaces, browser frames, locks, shields, collage, comparison board, or route/state label.

Produce the day master first. Produce night from the exact same composition: camera, geometry, roads, buildings, people, vehicles, vegetation, foreground, background and copy-safe area are fixed; only lighting, sky, lamps, windows, reflections and vehicle lights change. Default mobile focal point is bottom center; desktop copy-safe area is the quieter left third unless a route record says otherwise.

## Readiness checklist

Before integration, record two standalone files, matching composition, verified landmark/environment, no text/logos/state label, useful 1440/768/390/360 crops, day luminance > night luminance, meaningful perceptual difference, owner-review status, rights/cultural status, master/derivative SHA-256, dimensions and focal points. Any failure remains `blocked-missing-artwork`; a named landmark remains `pending-independent-landmark-verification` until reliable references are recorded.
