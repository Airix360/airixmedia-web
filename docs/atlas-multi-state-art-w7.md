# Atlas Multi-State Candidate Art W7

## Research and reasoning

Sources: the binding multi-state decision; complete Atlas foundation, world, visual-language, experience, illustration-production and website handoff documents; and W0–W6 implementation records. Plateau uses contemporary highland research terraces, granite, long sightlines, mist, field notes, maps and practical observation to express perspective without tourism. Ogun uses working thresholds, route choice, meeting courts, preparation registers, warm earth, timber and steel to express practical beginnings without industrial or transit spectacle.

## Generation method and status

OpenAI built-in image generation produced twelve candidates on 2026-07-14: independently authored desktop and mobile masters for Observatory (`ILL-0090`), Insights (`ILL-0091`), Resources (`ILL-0092`), Gateway (`ILL-0080`), Contact (`ILL-0081`) and Booking (`ILL-0082`). Desktop sources are 1672×941 PNGs. Mobile sources are 1003×1568 except Resources at 897×1752 and Booking at 971×1619. Desktop 1440×811, tablet 1024×768, mobile 960×1200 and thumbnail 480×270 WebPs were derived locally. Every package contains source, web, prompt, manifest and status records. All remain `candidate` behind explicit `internal-review` access.

## Visual vocabulary and safeguards

Approved: inhabited contemporary Nigerian architecture, organic ink, flat print colour, warm paper, restrained stone/green/earth accents, grounded people working naturally, blank records and copy-safe space. Prohibited: tourism, safari, wildlife decoration, colonial nostalgia, fantasy astronomy, mystical rocks, costume display, ceremonial gates, border cliché, factory smoke, endless trucks, call centres, handshakes, giant calendars/clocks, fake availability, generated logos, flags, readable text, photorealism, CGI, cyberpunk, floating UI and watermarks.

## Known defects and approval

Small hands, faces, map lines, papers, route marks, registers, materials and background geometry need close review. All locations are fictional composites. Abstract route surfaces may resemble unverified markings and must remain non-semantic. No separable motion layers exist. Source PNGs materially increase repository weight and require archive/LFS review. Owner, cultural, architectural, anatomy, crop, generated-text, rights/provenance, accessibility, and performance approval are required before promotion.
