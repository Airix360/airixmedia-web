Use a modern inhabited compound with shaded courtyard, restrained red-earth and mineral walls, contemporary masonry, timber shade, paper and clay surfaces, bronze-toned material accents, layered thresholds and colonnades. Show small anonymous contemporary Nigerian figures drawing, reviewing prototypes, comparing material samples, building, coding, testing, documenting and discussing around a long workshop table. Include blank review surfaces, document stacks and physical prototypes.

Use the Airix Atlas architectural editorial language: Gongbi-influenced precision, fine organic deep-brown or charcoal ink, simplified believable architecture, flat screen-printed or risograph-like colour, warm cream paper, restrained tonal shading and grounded human scale. Compose at eye level with clear depth and calm negative space on the left. Use measured blue hour and warm work light with cream, umber, charcoal, Edo red-brown, clay, timber, ochre and limited foliage green.

No readable text, generated logos, flags, sacred or royal objects, photorealism, cinematic 3D, futurism, generic African concept art, tourism treatment, palace fantasy, regalia, bronze props, masks, warriors, costume display, decorative patterns, neon, holograms, floating screens, fake language, watermark, cards or dashboards.
