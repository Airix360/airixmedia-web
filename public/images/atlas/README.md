# Airix Atlas Asset Library

Production assets for the Airix Atlas visual system.

## Structure

- `scenes/masters` — approved master scene candidates
- `scenes/responsive` — desktop, tablet, mobile, social and thumbnail crops
- `scenes/layers` — transparent depth layers for motion and compositing
- `scenes/candidates` — Gongbi, legacy and raw review candidates
- `objects` — 25 reusable Atlas objects in PNG and SVG
- `people`, `clothing`, `transport`, `vegetation`, `landmarks`, `street-details` — isolated production assets
- `markers` and `icons` — district and interface SVG systems
- `wayfinding` — route lines, nodes, arrows, labels, legends and templates
- `graphics` — stamps, labels, signs and lettering
- `textures` — paper, print, weather, environmental and shadow overlays
- `manifests` — machine-readable inventories
- `docs` — palette and usage guidance

## Visual direction

Gongbi-inspired technique + Lagos subject matter + Atlas palette.

Generated wording, public signage, logos and landmark details must be reviewed before publication.
