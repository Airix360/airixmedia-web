# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nontechnical decision-makers at journals, publishers, universities, research institutions, e-commerce businesses, international organisations, associations, startups, agencies, nonprofits, and public institutions. They need to understand what Airix can own, see credible proof, and submit a useful brief without learning implementation jargon.

## Product Purpose

Present Airix Media as a boutique creative technology studio and digital operations partner that builds, runs, and rescues the digital systems organisations rely on. The website should attract higher-value long-term work, preserve specialist publishing authority, increase recurring-service enquiries, and qualify prospects before consultation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, kinetic, premium. Confident, calm, human, African-founded, globally capable, and technically credible without being founder-centric.

## Anti-references

Generic agency templates, OJS-only positioning, founder-led portfolios, stock office photography, gradient-heavy SaaS pages, floating dashboards, rounded-card mosaics, Apple imitation, generic AI-network imagery, and unverified social proof.

## Design Principles

1. Explain business value before implementation detail.
2. Show connected systems rather than listing disconnected services.
3. Make long-term ownership and accountability visible.
4. Treat evidence status as part of the product, not an editorial afterthought.
5. Use motion to explain workflows and dependencies.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Honour reduced motion and system theme, preserve keyboard access and visible focus, avoid colour-only meaning, use plain language, and keep critical journeys translated before exposing a locale.
